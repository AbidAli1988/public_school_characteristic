This dataset contains detailed records of public school characteristics, including student-to-teacher ratios, school types, enrollment numbers, funding details, and additional educational factors. It focuses on data from public schools across various states and counties in the United States, providing insights into school performance, resource allocation, and demographic distributions.
# Motivation
The goal of this project is to understand the patterns and characteristics of public schools in different parts of the United States. Education is important for shaping the future of individuals and communities, and this dataset gives us the chance to look closely at how public schools work.

By studying this data, we hope to learn more about how things like school funding, the number of students per teacher, and the makeup of different student groups affect education. The dataset includes information like how many students are enrolled, what ethnic groups they belong to, what type of school they attend (such as charter or virtual), and how well the schools are performing.

Looking at these factors is important for understanding whether all students are getting the same quality of education, and if some schools or areas need more resources. By using this data, we can see how different types of schools in different regions serve their students and if certain groups face challenges due to a lack of resources or support.

In the end, this project aims to provide valuable information to policymakers, school leaders, and community members to help improve education, make sure resources are shared fairly, and create better strategies for improving schools.
